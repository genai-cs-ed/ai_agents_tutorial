Pick Your Task

Choose one thing you do over and over that you would happily hand off. The best pick is small, comes up often, and has a clear “done.” You will build on it for the rest of the session.

Fill in the four boxes in about four minutes.

What is it, in a few words? How often do you do it?
What do you start with? An email, notes, a spreadsheet, a form...
What do you end up with? A summary, a reply, a list, a report...
What would you check to be sure it was done well?

Pair share · 3 minutes

Explain your task to a neighbor, then listen to theirs. Ask one question about it.

Stuck? Ask yourself:

  • What do you copy, paste or retype from one place to another every week?
  • What email, summary or report do you write from the same template again and again?
  • What do you sort, tag, rename or check against a list?
  • What do you look up or gather from several places before the real work starts?

Keep it safe. Use a sanitized or made-up example today. Please do not use student records, health information, passwords, or confidential research data.
